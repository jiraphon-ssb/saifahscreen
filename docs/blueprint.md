# **App Name**: SAIFAH Design Studio

## Core Features:

- Product Catalog & Configuration: Display a range of customizable apparel products (e.g., Premium Cotton, Oversize Cotton T-shirts) with detailed descriptions, sizing, and material information. Users can select a product type.
- Interactive Design Tool: Provide a canvas where users can upload custom images, add text, and select from a library of pre-defined shapes or graphics to apply to their chosen apparel in real-time.
- Real-time Design Preview: Instantly display a visual mock-up of the designed product as modifications are made, allowing users to see their creation from various angles before purchase.
- AI Design Assistant Tool: Offer an AI tool that can suggest design elements, color palettes, or automatically generate simple graphic variations based on user text prompts (e.g., 'minimalist lightning icon', 'abstract speed pattern') to aid in the design process.
- User Account & Saved Designs: Allow users to create an account, save their design progress, and access a history of their past designs for future reordering or modifications.
- Shopping Cart & Checkout: Enable users to add their customized products to a shopping cart, review their order, and complete the purchase through a secure checkout process.
- Order Management & Tracking: Provide users with a dashboard to view their current order status, shipping information, and past order details.

## Style Guidelines:

- The brand focuses on 'speed,' 'precision,' and the 'power of lightning.' A dark color scheme best conveys power and allows vibrant highlights to pop. The primary color will be an energetic electric blue (#579BEA) for its dynamic and modern feel, representing the swift and precise nature of electricity.
- The background color is a deep, muted dark blue (#1A1D23). Its hue subtly aligns with the primary color, creating depth and a sophisticated, contemporary setting that enhances focus on design elements.
- A vibrant cyan accent color (#ACF7FF) will be used. Its bright, almost neon quality, analogous to the primary blue, is chosen for calls to action, highlights, and key interactive elements, reflecting the high energy and modernity of lightning.
- Headlines will use 'Space Grotesk' (sans-serif) for its modern, technical, and slightly futuristic vibe, perfectly capturing SAIFAH's emphasis on contemporary design and speed. Body text will use 'Inter' (sans-serif), a neutral, highly readable font, ensuring clarity and professionalism for all detailed information.
- Icons should be clean, geometric, and modern, often utilizing a subtle lightning motif or sharp angles to reflect the brand's 'power of lightning' theme. Prioritize SVG icons for crispness and scalability across all devices.
- Embrace a clean, responsive layout with a strong focus on intuitive navigation and spacious design to highlight products and the customization experience. Prioritize visual hierarchy to guide users through the design process effortlessly.
- Implement subtle, swift animations for UI interactions (e.g., button hovers, section transitions, real-time preview updates). These animations should be fast and fluid, conveying the brand's core value of 'speed' and enhancing the overall user experience without causing delays.
